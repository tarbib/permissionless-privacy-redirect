Automatic redirects in Brave

This extension can't see the links you click, but Brave itself can run a small script on pages. Set it up once and links to the sites you've turned on open on their frontend automatically. The extension still has no permissions.

  1. Open brave://settings/shields/filters and turn on Developer mode at the bottom of the page.

Pages on a supported site that you open some other way, such as from another app, switch to the frontend as soon as they start loading, so that site still sees the first request. Come back here and update both after changing your instances.
